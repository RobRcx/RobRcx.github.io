About me

Hi! I am Roberto Ronco, a researcher in combinatorial optimization and algebraic graph theory.

I graduated summa cum laude in Information Engineering at the University of Genoa, Italy, in 2018, and received my Ph.D. in Operations Research from the same university in February 2022.

My research interests include combinatorial optimization, graph and matroid theory, discrete optimization, and their applications.
